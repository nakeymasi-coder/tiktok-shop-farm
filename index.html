Your control panel for TikTok and TikTok Shop videos - intake, Character Vault, solo agent jobs, and your run Queue, all in one place for @glamorousgrammyglowup.

Plain truth first: this dashboard is your control panel only - intake, vault, and queue. How this farm works

Fill out the Video Intake Form (or a solo agent form), and the job lands in your Queue. Open it, tap Run in Chat, copy the prompt, and paste it into chat. Your AI runs the farm WITH you in chat - Pinterest outfit or TikTok Shop product, locked character, story, video, captions - and brings the finished video back in chat for your approval. There is no scheduled pickup.

The Queue status buttons (queued / working / done) only change the label. They never execute anything. Execution happens in chat.

The 6 agents - in order

1. Pinterest Scout

Searches Pinterest (guest view, no sign-in) for outfits and products for the chosen mode and topic, especially products with an Amazon link. Picks the outfit or product and copies its picture. Does not check price or availability at this step.

2. TikTok Shop Agent

Searches TikTok Shop and picks the best products for you to make TikTok Shop videos about - demo-friendly, camera-friendly products that fit your audience. For each pick it reports the product name, what the product picture shows, why it fits, the TikTok Shop product link exactly as shown, and the price exactly as shown on the listing, never a guess. Never buys, orders, or adds anything to a cart. On a TikTok Shop run it leads instead of the Pinterest Scout, and its pick feeds the story, the script, and the video.

3. Image Agent

Creates the person and avatar images in chat - character references, the outfit photo (your locked character wearing the exact Pinterest outfit, or presenting the exact TikTok Shop product), and the outfit reference sheet. All ultra realistic, real skin texture and real hair texture, all PNG, all 9:16.

4. Story Agent

Writes the hook, the shots, and the script. Labels every scene with the run's one Product Presentation choice. Writes scene ACTION only for video prompts - never a description of the character.

5. Video Agent

Makes the video in chat with your AI video tool, using the Consistent Character workflow below. Returns the finished video in chat for your approval. Never posts it.

6. Caption Agent

Writes the TikTok caption, the on-screen hook, and hashtags for TikTok and TikTok Shop. Ends on the benefit plus tap the product link. No price, no sold counts, no discount or sale claim - ever - in a caption.

Product Presentation - one choice, never both

Every run picks ONE, right after Mode: Wearing the product OR Holding the product. Never both.

Wearing run - every scene prompt ends: "Wearing the product, not holding it."
Holding run - every scene prompt ends: "Holding the product, not wearing it."

Never show her wearing an outfit while holding that same outfit on a hanger. Reject and regenerate any image that shows both. If a video needs both, use separate scenes - holding first, wearing later - never the same shot.

The workflow - every full run, in order

1. Pick the outfit on Pinterest (one with an Amazon link when there is a product). Copy its picture. For a TikTok Shop run, the TikTok Shop Agent picks the product first instead, and that pick feeds everything after it.

2. Lock the outfit IN CHAT, not in the video tool: using your locked character's reference photos, put that character in that exact outfit (or presenting that exact product) in chat first. Check face match and outfit or product match.

3. Make the outfit reference sheet IN CHAT: from the approved outfit photo, one single 9:16 sheet with front, profile, and three-quarter shots of her in that outfit, all on the same photo. Save as PNG.

4. Build the video with the Consistent Character lock: Reference One is her headshot (Slot 1). Reference Two is the chat-made outfit reference sheet. NEVER leave Reference Two empty. No character bible is needed - the reference photos ARE the lock. Do not paste descriptions of her into scene prompts.

5. Create the scenes from that lock, in script order, 9:16.

6. Export and return the video IN CHAT for your approval. Never post, publish, or share it.

The scene prompt shape - use this, and nothing else about her

"Use the reference images and keep the character perfectly consistent. [Scene action only.] [Spoken line, if any.] Wearing the product, not holding it."

For a Holding run, the last sentence swaps to: "Holding the product, not wearing it."

NEVER describe her in a scene prompt - no face, hair, skin, body, or outfit description. Describing her overrides the references and creates a different girl.

Standing rules

- Every image your AI creates is PNG, never webp. Your own uploaded Vault photos stay as you saved them (JPG or PNG). A webp upload gets rejected with "JPG or PNG please."

- Nothing is posted automatically, ever. Posting always needs your separate approval.

- No price, no sold counts, no discount or sale claim in any video (spoken or on-screen) or caption. Prices change, a stale price reads as false advertising, and that risks a TikTok Shop violation. The TikTok Shop product link shows the live price. The TikTok Shop Agent may report a price exactly as shown only inside its pick report in chat - that price never goes into a video or caption.

- The TikTok Shop Agent never buys, orders, or adds anything to a cart. It only picks and reports.

- Characters are ultra realistic, with real skin texture and real hair texture. Within one video, identity, clothing, location, and lighting stay frozen from the reference photos.

- Voice and copy everywhere: personable, human, plain words. No em dashes - hyphens only.
